# Company Z Global Expense Policy

## Section 1: Travel Expenses
- **Clause 1.1 (Flights):** All domestic and international flights must be booked in Economy class. Business class is strictly prohibited.
- **Clause 1.2 (Accommodation):** Hotel stays are capped at $200 per night. Any amount exceeding this limit requires explicit Vice President approval before booking.
- **Clause 1.3 (Ground Transport):** Taxis and ride-sharing services are fully claimable for travel between the airport, hotel, and client sites.

## Section 2: Meal Allowances
- **Clause 2.1 (Daily Per Diem):** Employees are granted a maximum meal allowance of $50 per day while traveling for business.
- **Clause 2.2 (Client Dinners):** Meals involving external clients are capped at $100 per person.
- **Clause 2.3 (Alcohol):** The purchase of alcohol is strictly non-claimable and constitutes a policy violation under all circumstances.

## Section 3: Software and Subscriptions
- **Clause 3.1 (Standard Software):** Monthly software subscriptions under $50 do not require pre-approval.
- **Clause 3.2 (Enterprise Software):** Software purchases or subscriptions exceeding $50 must be drafted and submitted for line manager approval prior to purchase.

## Section 4: Office Supplies
- **Clause 4.1 (General Supplies):** Employees may claim up to $100 per calendar quarter for remote-work office supplies (e.g., keyboards, notebooks, cables).
- **Clause 4.2 (Hardware):** Laptops and monitors cannot be expensed via corporate cards and must be procured directly through the IT Department.

## Section 5: General Claim Guidelines
- **Clause 5.1 (Submission Window):** All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction date.
- **Clause 5.2 (Duplicate Claims):** Submitting multiple claims for the exact same transaction ID or receipt is a severe policy violation.
